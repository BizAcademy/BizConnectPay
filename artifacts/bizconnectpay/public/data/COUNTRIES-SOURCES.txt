Small country flag PNG images: https://flagcdn.com/
https://flagpedia.net/download/api
Images are stored locally; visitor browsers do not contact the flag service.

The catalog contains 250 countries and territories, including Kosovo.
Antarctica (AQ) and Heard Island and McDonald Islands (HM) have no assigned
country calling code in this catalog. They are labeled accordingly rather than
being assigned an invented telephone prefix.
Countries sharing an international calling code remain separate ISO choices.
